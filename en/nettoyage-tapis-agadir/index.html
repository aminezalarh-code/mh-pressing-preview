Rug cleaning in Agadir

M&H Pressing cleans rugs and carpets in Agadir, from 30 DH. The final price depends on the size, the material and the condition of the rug. We collect from your home and return it clean and dry.

Book a pickup WhatsApp

A rug holds dust deep in the pile, well beyond what a vacuum lifts. Cleaning therefore starts with dust extraction, then a treatment matched to the material and the way the rug is woven.

Wool, cotton and synthetic fibres do not react the same way to water and products. We examine the rug before choosing the treatment, which matters most for older or handmade pieces.

Drying is complete before return: a rug given back damp will develop a smell and can mark the floor. That is why it takes longer than everyday laundry.

What the service covers

  • Dust extraction before cleaning
  • Treatment matched to material and weave
  • Wool, cotton or synthetic rugs
  • Fitted carpets
  • Dried through before return
  • Price confirmed after inspecting the rug

Who it is for

  • Heavily used living-room rugs
  • Seasonal deep clean
  • After a spill or a spreading stain
  • Furnished rentals and guesthouses
