Frequently asked

Your questions, answered.

Where is M&H Pressing in Agadir?

Our shop is at Ilot, Haut Founty, in Agadir (postal code 80000). You can drop off and collect your laundry in person, or request a home pickup.

What are the opening hours?

We are open Monday to Saturday, from 09:00 to 20:00. The shop is closed on Sunday.

Do you offer home pickup and delivery?

Yes. We collect your laundry from your address and bring it back clean and pressed. The service covers Agadir and the surrounding area. You book a slot from the form on this site or directly on WhatsApp.

How long does the service take?

The standard turnaround is 24 to 48 hours, depending on the type and volume. Bulky items such as rugs or duvets can take longer; we confirm this when we collect. If you are in a hurry, the express service allows processing in under 24 hours with a 50% surcharge.

How do I book a pickup?

Fill in the booking form on this page with your neighbourhood and your preferred date and time. Your request is recorded, then the conversation continues on WhatsApp to confirm the details. You can also message us directly on WhatsApp at 06 42 07 01 96.

How much does home delivery cost?

Delivery within Agadir is 50 DH. Around Agadir it is 100 DH, including areas such as Taghazout and Tamraght. Tell us your area when you book and we confirm the price before coming.

Do you deliver to Taghazout and Tamraght?

Yes. Taghazout and Tamraght are among the areas around Agadir that we serve, at a delivery price of 100 DH. Message us on WhatsApp at 06 42 07 01 96 to arrange a slot.

Is delivery free?

No, delivery is paid: 50 DH in Agadir and 100 DH in the surrounding area. This is charged on top of the price for treating your items.

Do you offer an express service in under 24 hours?

Yes. Your items can be processed in less than 24 hours with a 50% surcharge. Ask for the express service when you book or on WhatsApp at 06 42 07 01 96: we check what the item needs and confirm the turnaround before taking your laundry.

How much does the express service cost?

The express service adds a 50% surcharge. Because our prices vary from item to item, the exact amount depends on your order: we confirm it before starting.

Do you clean curtains, and do you take them down?

Yes to both. We clean nets, lined and blackout curtains, and we can come and take them down then return to put them back up once clean. Prices start at 20 DH per curtain washed and ironed.

Do you do dry cleaning?

Yes. M&H Pressing provides dry cleaning in Agadir for pieces that will not survive washing in water: suits, structured jackets, coats, formal dresses and traditional wear. A full suit is 40 DH.

What are your prices?

Our full price list is published on this site, item by item. For example, a shirt is 15 DH for wash and iron, or 8 DH for ironing only. Prices are in Moroccan dirhams.

Which fabrics and items do you handle?

We handle everyday clothing, traditional wear (caftans, takchitas, djellabas), home linen (sheets, duvets, curtains), rugs and carpets, and leather and suede. We offer dry cleaning, washing and ironing.

What does “Giant Washing Machine” mean?

“Giant Washing Machine” is part of the M&H Pressing brand identity: it is the name on our storefront in Haut Founty, alongside M&H. Both refer to the same dry cleaner, at the same address in Agadir.
