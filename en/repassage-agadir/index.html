Ironing in Agadir

M&H Pressing offers an ironing service in Agadir, in Haut Founty. You can have clean laundry pressed without paying for washing: ironing only starts at 4 DH per item, and we can collect and deliver to your door.

Book a pickup WhatsApp

Ironing only makes sense when you wash at home but have neither the time nor the inclination to press. You hand over clean laundry and we return it folded or on hangers depending on the item.

Shirts, trousers, dresses and suits are steam pressed at a temperature matched to the fabric. Traditional wear — djellabas, caftans, takchitas — is handled separately, with the finishing done by hand.

If the laundry has not been washed yet, the "wash + iron" price applies and covers both. Both columns sit side by side in our price list.

What the service covers

  • Steam pressing at a fabric-appropriate temperature
  • Shirts returned on hangers
  • Trousers and suits with a set crease
  • Traditional wear finished by hand
  • Item-by-item check before return
  • Garment covers for formal pieces

Who it is for

  • People who wash at home but do not iron
  • Professionals who need shirts ready each week
  • Families ahead of a celebration
  • Anyone in a hurry before travelling

Frequently asked

Your questions, answered.

How much does ironing cost in Agadir at M&H?

Ironing only starts at 4 DH for a garment: a shirt or a pair of trousers is 8 DH, a dress 10 DH, a suit 20 DH. Table linen goes lower — a small napkin is 2 DH. The full list is published on this site.

Can I have laundry I washed myself ironed?

Yes — that is exactly what this service is for. You give us clean, dry laundry and we charge for pressing only.

Do you iron caftans and takchitas?

Yes. Ironing a djellaba is 10 DH and a takchita 20 DH. These pieces are handled separately and finished by hand.

How long does ironing take?

The standard turnaround is 24 to 48 hours. Tell us your deadline when you book and we will say whether it is achievable.
