Curtain cleaning in Agadir

M&H Pressing cleans curtains in Agadir, from 20 DH washed and ironed or 10 DH for ironing alone. We can also handle taking them down and putting them back up: we come and unhook your curtains, treat them at our workshop in Haut Founty, and return to re-hang them.

Book a pickup WhatsApp

This is the part that puts most people off: unhooking curtains, carrying them, then getting them back up straight. We take that step on. No stepladder to fetch, no rail to dismantle — we come, we take them down, and we come back to hang them once they are clean.

Treatment depends heavily on the fabric: a light net, a lined curtain and a blackout curtain are neither washed nor pressed the same way. We check the composition before choosing a method, particularly for linings, which can shrink.

Curtains are pressed flat and returned ready to go back up. Where we handle the re-hanging, you find them already hung, without the storage creases.

Tell us the number of curtains, their approximate height and the type of rail when you book: that lets us plan the visit and confirm the terms with you.

What the service covers

  • Curtains taken down at your home
  • Nets, lined and blackout curtains
  • Tablecloths and table linen
  • Fabric checked before treatment
  • Pressed flat after washing
  • Re-hung and put back in place after cleaning

Who it is for

  • Spring cleaning
  • After building work or a move
  • Homes on a busy street
  • Anyone who would rather not climb a stepladder

Frequently asked

Your questions, answered.

Do you take curtains down and put them back up?

Yes. We can come and unhook your curtains, take them to our workshop, and return to put them back once they are clean. Mention it when you book so we can plan the visit.

How much does curtain cleaning cost in Agadir?

From 20 DH washed and ironed, or 10 DH for ironing only, per curtain. That is a starting price: size and fabric change the final figure, which we confirm after inspection.

Do you clean nets and blackout curtains?

Yes, but not in the same way. We check the composition and the lining before choosing a treatment, because some linings shrink when washed.

What should I tell you when booking?

The number of curtains, their approximate height and the type of rail. That lets us plan the visit. For anything about the terms, message us on WhatsApp.
