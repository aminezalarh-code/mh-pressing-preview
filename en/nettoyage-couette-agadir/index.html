Duvet cleaning in Agadir

M&H Pressing cleans duvets, blankets and throws in Agadir. A single duvet is 30 DH and a double 40 DH. We collect from your home and bring it back clean and dry.

Book a pickup WhatsApp

A duvet will not go into a domestic machine without being compressed, which stops it rinsing and drying properly. We have large-capacity machines and suitable drying, which avoids the residual damp and the smell that comes with it.

The filling is redistributed after washing so the duvet regains its loft instead of staying in clumps. Thick blankets and salon blankets are handled separately, at their own price.

Expect a longer turnaround than everyday laundry: drying a duvet through takes time, and we never return a piece that is still damp. We confirm the timing when we collect.

What the service covers

  • Single and double duvets
  • Blankets, throws and salon blankets
  • Thick blankets
  • Washed in large-capacity machines
  • Dried through before return
  • Filling redistributed to restore loft

Who it is for

  • Changing season or putting winter bedding away
  • After a long spell in storage
  • Furnished rentals between stays
  • Homes without a large-capacity machine
