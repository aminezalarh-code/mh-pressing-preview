Frequently asked

Your questions, answered.

Are prices the same in the shop and at home?

Yes. The treatment prices published here apply equally to a shop drop-off and a home pickup.

What does "from" mean?

That the price depends on the piece: its size, its fabric, its finishing. That applies to rugs, curtains, caftans and takchitas. We confirm the price after inspection, before starting.

What is the difference between the two columns?

"Wash + iron" covers both operations. "Ironing" applies when the laundry is already clean and only needs pressing, and costs less.
