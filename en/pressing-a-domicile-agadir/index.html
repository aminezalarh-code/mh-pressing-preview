Laundry pickup and delivery in Agadir

M&H Pressing offers laundry pickup and delivery in Agadir: you book a slot, a courier collects from your address, we treat everything at our workshop in Haut Founty, then bring it back clean and pressed. The service covers Agadir and the surrounding area.

Book a pickup WhatsApp

You pick the date and time that suit you from the form on this site, or straight on WhatsApp. Tell us your neighbourhood, your address and what is involved: clothes, duvets, curtains, rugs or something else.

Everything is sorted at the workshop, treated according to its fabric, pressed, checked and then protected for the journey back. The standard turnaround is 24 to 48 hours; we confirm the return slot when we collect.

The whole conversation happens on WhatsApp, from the first message to the delivery. There is no app to install, and your request is recorded with us the moment you submit the form.

What the service covers

  • Collection at the address and slot you choose
  • Clothing and home linen both handled
  • Treatment at the Haut Founty workshop
  • Delivery clean, pressed and protected
  • Order followed up on WhatsApp
  • Treatment prices identical to those displayed in the shop
  • Delivery: 50 DH in Agadir, 100 DH in the surrounding area (Taghazout and Tamraght)

Who it is for

  • People working during the shop’s opening hours
  • Families with a large volume of laundry
  • Agadir residents without a car
  • Rental owners between stays

How we work

  1. You book

    Through the form on this site or on WhatsApp. Give us the neighbourhood, address and slot.

  2. We collect

    An M&H courier comes to the address at the agreed time.

  3. We treat

    Sorting, cleaning or washing by fabric, then pressing and checking at the workshop.

  4. We deliver

    Back to your door, clean, pressed and protected for transport.

Frequently asked

Your questions, answered.

Which parts of Agadir do you cover?

We pick up and deliver across Agadir and the surrounding area. Our workshop is in Haut Founty. Give us your neighbourhood when you book and we will confirm the slot.

How do I book a pickup?

Fill in the booking form on this site with your neighbourhood and preferred date and time. Your request is recorded, then the conversation continues on WhatsApp to confirm details. You can also message us directly on WhatsApp.

Is it more expensive than coming to the shop?

Treatment prices are the ones published on this site, the same as in the shop. Delivery is charged separately: 50 DH in Agadir and 100 DH in the surrounding area, including places such as Taghazout and Tamraght.

What if I am not there when the courier arrives?

Let us know on WhatsApp as soon as you can and we will reschedule. It is the same conversation throughout.
